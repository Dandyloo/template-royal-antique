About Us

Three decades of craft, care, and commitment to the finest Ghanaian furniture.

We started with a belief.

Royal Antique Home was founded in 2026 as a subsidiary of Kings Group with a simple purpose — to bring timeless royal antique furniture into modern Ghanaian homes.
We exist for people who value elegance, quality, and furniture that stands the test of time.
Based in Spintex, Accra, we specialise in imported royal antique furniture carefully selected to elevate every space — from bedrooms and living rooms to dining areas, kitchen sets, and statement pieces designed to define a home.

Craft as philosophy.

We believe furniture should do more than fill a room.
It should express taste, heritage, and attention to detail.
Every piece we offer is chosen for its design, structure, and finish — combining classic European inspiration with the comfort and practicality of modern living. Our collections are made to bring warmth, sophistication, and long-term value into everyday spaces.

A commitment to Quality.

We do not believe in disposable furniture.
Each product is selected for its durability, detailing, and ability to complement both traditional and contemporary interiors.
From sourcing to showroom experience, we take a careful and deliberate approach — ensuring every piece meets our expectations for design, balance, and strength.
